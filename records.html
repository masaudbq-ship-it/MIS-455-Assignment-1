Moments every Tigers fan remembers.

Proud moments

The Tigers started with heavy defeats, but they kept learning. Over the years they have beaten the best teams in the world at home, and in 2024 they won a Test series in Pakistan, something many fans once thought impossible.

The table below lists a few milestones. Cricket numbers change after every match, so check ESPNcricinfo for the latest figures.

YearMilestone
2000Bangladesh becomes a Test nation and plays its first Test, against India in Dhaka.
2010Tamim Iqbal scores a Test century at Lord's.
2013Mushfiqur Rahim becomes the first Bangladeshi to score a Test double century.
2015Bangladesh reaches the World Cup quarter-finals for the first time.
2016First Test win over England, at Mirpur.
2017First Test win over Australia, also at Mirpur.
2024Bangladesh wins a Test series in Pakistan 2-0.

Full stats: ESPNcricinfo Statsguru · ICC
