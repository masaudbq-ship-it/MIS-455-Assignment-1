The Stadium

Where the Mirpur crowd turns up the volume.

Mirpur: home of the Tigers

The Sher-e-Bangla National Cricket Stadium in Mirpur, Dhaka, is the main home of Bangladesh cricket. It opened in 2006 and holds around 25,000 spectators. It hosted the opening match of the 2011 World Cup between Bangladesh and India, and the final of the 2014 World T20.

Bangladesh also plays at the Zahur Ahmed Chowdhury Stadium in Chattogram, a port city of hills and sea, and at the Sylhet International Cricket Stadium, set among the green hills of the north-east. Each ground has its own mood, but the noise from the stands is the same everywhere.
